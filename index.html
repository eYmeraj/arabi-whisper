How to read the sounds

Words are spelled the way Egyptian singers say them. Double letters mean a longer sound.

Consonants
'a catch in the throat: the squeezed sound of ع, or the stop in "uh-oh" (ء, and ق in Cairo)ma'aak with you
hboth ح (a strong breath from the throat) and هhena here
khlike "ch" in Scottish "loch"khalaas enough
ghlike a French "r"ghaali dear
galways hard, as in "go" (ج)gameel beautiful
rrolled, as in Spanishrooh soul

s-htwo sounds, not "sh"; the same for t‑h, k‑h, g‑h and d‑has-ha I wake up
